AIS points seem to be received by VesselVoyage out of order. We expect them to be in order given that VesselVoyage consumes the AisDiff messages which cannot be received out of order. When looking in ShipHistory it seems that the AIS got received just fine, so something weird is going on here.

This could be:

# A problem on the side of AisStream?
# A problem on the side of NATS?

As counter measure some kind of mechanism could be made into VesselVoyage to ensure we get the messages in order.
