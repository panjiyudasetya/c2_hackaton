## Description

h2. Description

After going through a couple of Visits when you look for their ETA prediction it is most of the time the case that they are for a time in the past.

Most likely the updating of the ETA is not being done correctly or the predictor doesn’t provide an updated ETA in the NATS KV?

Example ship where I would’ve expected an ETA → [https://portcallone.com/en/vessels/9175781|https://portcallone.com/en/vessels/9175781|smart-link] 
But this can be done for any big ship.

Find out what is going wrong and then lets discuss what way forward we want to take as this might become too big of an issue depending on what is going wrong.

h2. Dependencies

Almost no ETAs are shown in PortCallOne that are from Teqplay and it seems to be always failing back to the AIS eta which is not something we want.
