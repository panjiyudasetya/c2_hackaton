## Description

h2. Description

This seems to be a problem on VesselVoyage and ETA predictor side. With VesselVoyage still containing old predictions from the past and ETA predictor not updating their ETA.

Example voyage: [https://vesselvoyagedev.teqplay.nl/#/ships/9292773/story?mode=period&months=3|https://vesselvoyagedev.teqplay.nl/#/ships/9292773/story?mode=period&months=3|smart-link] 

Current ETA is on 6th of dec 2025 even though this date is a month in the past and the ship is currently sailing towards BRSSZ (santos).

h2. Dependencies

Direct impact on PortCallOne DEV (with some cases also happening on PROD).
