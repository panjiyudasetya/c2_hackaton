## Description

h2. Impact

The bug affects the display of vessel visits at Yara Sluiskil. Users see visits without any voyages in between.

h2. Expected behaviour

The system should correctly display voyages between visits, ensuring that all vessel movements are accurately represented.

h2. Actual behaviour

Currently, the system shows vessels visiting Yara Sluiskil without any recorded voyages in between. It appears that vessels are returning without leaving, despite actually being in another port in the UK.

h2. Steps to reproduce

* Run revents to recalculate by port NLTNZ.
* After merging back into VesselVoyage Visits are created by in-between Voyages are not created.
