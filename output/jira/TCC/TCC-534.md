## Description

h2. Description

Lets first switch DEV to this new URL, start of the next sprint, lets check stability during the sprint and if all is good then lets switch production.

They did provide a hard date for the transition on the 1st of December so we need to be over by that time.

The email received from Spire:

{quote}Dear customer,

As part of our ongoing migration from Spire Global to Kpler, we are updating the configuration for our Spire Maritime TCP feed. This change is required to ensure uninterrupted service under Kpler’s infrastructure.

Your systems are currently receiving streaming NMEA data from [streamingv2.ais.spire.com|http://streamingv2.ais.spire.com]. This service will be decommissioned, and we ask that you connect to [streaming.sml.kpler.com|http://streaming.sml.kpler.com] port 25000 instead.

Please note that your existing network policies may permit access to the legacy service but not to the new endpoint. To ensure connectivity to the new service, update your firewall settings to allow outbound traffic to port 25000 on the following IP addresses:

63.34.104.226

54.195.92.196

52.51.219.17

During the migration process, please maintain your existing client connections to the legacy service until the new configuration has been fully implemented and confirmed stable. This will help prevent any loss of data during the transition. Please complete your transition at the latest by 1st December 2025.

Kindly note that this notice applies solely to the Spire Maritime TCP product. All other services in the portfolio will continue to operate from their current addresses at this time.

Thank you for your prompt action and for being a valued partner during this migration. If you have any questions or need assistance, our support team is here to help at [sp@kpler.com|mailto:sp@kpler.com]

 

Regards,

The Kpler Support team{quote}

h2. Definition of Done

* Develop changed to new URL and port
* Changes are stable and no impact on AIS coverage
* Production changed to new URL and port
* Checked that DEV and PROD have the same amount of data
