## Description

When a vessel enters the port of Singapore, and goes anchored there are many pilot events, for example:

{quote}a) when entering the port area to sail towards the anchorage

b) when arrived in the anchorage pilot will disembark

*c) Just before leaving the anchorage to sail to the berth*

d) when arrived at berth the pilot will disembark

e) before shifting away from the berth the pilot will embark

f) arriving at the anchorage for the shift the pilot will disembark

g) just before departure the anchorage the pilot will embark

h) arriving at the terminal, the pilot will disembark

i) Just before leaving the terminal the pilot will embark

*j) when anchoring before departure the pilot will disembark at the anchorage*

k) when departing anchorage to sail the pilot will embark

m) when vessel sails out of the port the pilot will disembark.{quote}

!image (27).png|width=911,height=410!

We agreed with APMT to make the pilot on board (relevant for pilot-to-pilot timeframe) as moment C defined above, and take moment J as the pilot disembarked (or if the vessel did not go anchored while sailing that would be moment M).
