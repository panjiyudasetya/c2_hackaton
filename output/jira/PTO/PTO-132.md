## Description

If 2 were active i intend to pick the first one for now, so that we have the voyage information available with that mmsi



Vessels change their mmsi. It looks like PTO ETL does always provide the latest MMSI related to the specific IMO, instead of providing the MMSI relevant by then. Biggest issue is here related to the timeline URL, which does not open relevant information.
