# [PTO-216] As a PTO user I want to be able to generate reports covering also ports that we did not know properly before WorldOfPorts

**Board:** PTO board  

## Description

Had an initial envisioning about this, where we discussed how to realize. In general we agreed that the dependency of (r)events and PTO on vesselvoyage should not be changed, but the contents of vesselvoyage should be updated as much as possible. 

Maurice came with the idea to generate a ‘parallel universum' version of (r)events and (re)vesselvoyage in which a complete new picture will be calculated composed of consistent events and consistent Vesselvoyage visits,stops and voyages. 

Such environment could be used to make a clear cut in a place where we could assume least processes are taking place (e.g. open sea before and after port entrance/exit). So, a consistent period could be copied over (events + voyages) into the real environment.
