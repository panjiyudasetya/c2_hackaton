# [PTO-254] Make sure when a vessel goes slow steaming after getting anchored near USLAX , it will be counted as waiting time outside port

## Description

in LA port we noticed multiple times a vessel arriving at anchorage, and going slow steaming after, then going anchored again before entering the port. The total time when not moving (at the anchorage, hovering slowly (while not leaving the EOS)) should be counted as part of the ‘waiting time outside port’. Sailing time will not be included in there.

Currently this time only becomes visible by deducting  {{anchor to pilot}} - {{pilot to pilot}}
