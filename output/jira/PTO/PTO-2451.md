## Description

With the current ingestion pipeline, I have the problem that i can not really recalculate a shorter period. If I recalculate a month, when ingesting, I would need to clear datawarehouse and datamart, and it means that I am losing the periods where I didn’t recalculate.



The only option I have left is to recalculate the whole period I am interested in. This is usually full 2 years.
