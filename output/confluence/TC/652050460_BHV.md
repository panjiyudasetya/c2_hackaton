### BHV Company

* **Name**: <http://101BHV.nl>
* **Location**: Barbizonlaan 25, 2908 MB Capelle aan den IJssel, Nederland

This is a cursus for people who do not have a BHV certificate yet. This cursus is for small company's and ZZP'ers. You do not need to have any experience in First Aid.

In this cursus you will learn all theoretical and practical elements for BHV. After this day you know what to do in situations of accidents, fire or clearance.

**Content BHV basic**

Part 1: First Aid

* Approaching victim(s)
* Assessing consciousness
* Reanimate with AED
* Stable side positioning
* Suffocation
* Wat to do when a victim has injuries or burns
* Symptoms of illness
* Treating injuries

Part 2: fire and clearance

* The tasks of a BHV'er
* Reporting incidents
* The role of professional aid workers
* When to put out a fire
* How to put out different fires correctly
* How extinguishing gear works
* Evacuation procedures
