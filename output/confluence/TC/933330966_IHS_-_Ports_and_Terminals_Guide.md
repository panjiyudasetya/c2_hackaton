# IHS - Ports and Terminals Guide

**Space:** TC  
**URL:** https://teqplaybv.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TC/pages/933330966  

## Content

IHS is a reference for ports, terminals, berths, and related services worldwide. The contents include:

* Detailed entries for **ports**, **terminals** and **berths** worldwide.
* Pre-arrival and **navigation (anchorage area, pilot boarding place, approach routes, etc)** and operational information.

However, **the information regarding terminal and berth could be outdated**, so we need to crosscheck it with actual data from the port's official website and other sources.
