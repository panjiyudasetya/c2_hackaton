# World Of Ports Models

**Space:** TC  
**URL:** https://teqplaybv.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TC/pages/211222529  

## Content

World Of Ports only uses 3 main models in their world of Ports application: Port, Terminal and Berth. The definition of those models seems to be covering multiple usages though. This section describes the different types of use-cases used for the 3 different models. The details about the world of ports models are described in [their interface specifications](https://teqplaybv.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/214269982/2023_10_10_WorldOfPortsAPIUserGuide.pdf?api=v2).
