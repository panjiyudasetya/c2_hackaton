# GenAI adoption for Processes

**Space:** TC  
**URL:** https://teqplaybv.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TC/pages/1022951425  

## Content

This is a central hub to help Teqplay teams adopt, use, and scale Generative AI in daily workflows, centralizing everything related to **AI-powered process improvements** at Teqplay.  
Here you’ll find:

* **Resources** – reusable GPTs, assistant tools, and knowledge assets
* **Use Cases** – validated applications of AI in our processes
* **Integrations** – AI embedded into tools (Power BI, Confluence, monday.com | The AI work platform , etc.)

The goal is to make it **easy for teams to discover, test, and adopt AI solutions** that improve efficiency, decision quality, and output consistency. Templates can be viewed and edited on the [link](https://teqplaybv.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TC/settings/look-and-feel/templates).
