# Teqplay Policies and Processes

**Space:** TC  
**URL:** https://teqplaybv.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TC/pages/649789444  

## Content

Here we document all policies and processes within Teqplay. The following policies are planned:

#### 1. **Security Policies**

* **Information Security Policy** – Outlines overall security practices, responsibilities, and controls.
* **Access Control Policy** – Defines user access management, least privilege principles, and authentication (MFA, SSO).
* **Password Policy** – Ensures strong password enforcement, complexity, and rotation.
* **Incident Response Policy** – Details how security incidents are detected, reported, and remediated.
* **Risk Management Policy** – Identifies risks, assessment methodologies, and mitigation strategies.
* **Vendor Risk Management Policy** – Governs third-party security, compliance, and due diligence.
* **Encryption Policy** – Defines how data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

#### 2. **Availability Policies**

* **Disaster Recovery (DR) & Business Continuity (BC) Plan** – Ensures resilience and uptime guarantees.
* **System Monitoring & Logging Policy** – Specifies how system performance, logs, and security alerts are monitored.

Additionally, the following processes are planned to be described and worked out in more detail:

* **Access Reviews & User Management** – Regular reviews of employee and third-party access to critical systems.
* **Security Awareness Training** – Ongoing training on security best practices and compliance requirements.
* **Incident Response Testing** – Running tabletop exercises and testing security incident response plans.
* **Change Management Process** – Ensuring secure deployment of code and infrastructure changes.
* **Continuous Monitoring & Auditing** – Implementing logging, SIEM (Security Information and Event Management), and anomaly detection.
