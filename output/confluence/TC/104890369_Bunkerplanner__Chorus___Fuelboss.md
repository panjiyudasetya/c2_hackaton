## Content

Helloo, and welcome at Bunkerplanner, Chorus, Fuelboss.

Let’s do some terminology:

* **Bunkerplanner** is the internal name of the project
* **Chorus** is the back-end + front-end developed by us, for Shell
* **Fuelboss** is a product of DNV, using our the back-end API + front-end component/utility library

attention!Blue

The back-end project has been split up in June 2023. The back-end project is split into separate repositories: `chorus-backend` and `fuelboss-backend`. The front-end will also split up, but is not done at the time of writing.
