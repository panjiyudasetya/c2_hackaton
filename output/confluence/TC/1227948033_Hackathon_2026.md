# Set the course with AI — turning maritime data into action

For 24 hours we step away from the day-to-day to build something new together, with mixed teams across Ofiniti and Teqplay all working towards a shared theme. It’s about trying things, learning fast and having fun doing it — fake it till you make it, then show it off.

## What we want to get out of it

Teamwork — working together across offices, disciplines and time zones.  
Problem solving — taking a real maritime challenge and cracking it under time pressure.  
Innovation — experimenting with ideas and tools we don’t normally get to touch.

## Theme & example directions

###### Assignment

Build an AI-powered solution that turns Teqplay data into action — surfacing insights, proposing or simulating strategies, and helping ships, ports and terminals make smarter real-time decisions on safety, efficiency and sustainability.

Need a starting point? A few directions from our brainstorms:

* ***Bunkering operations*** — predict when and where vessels will refuel, match bunker barges to ships, and flag clashes or delays before they cascade.
* ***LNG bunkering*** — as fleets switch to LNG, map demand and infrastructure readiness and simulate safe scheduling around the larger safety zones it needs.
* ***Port & terminal benchmarking*** — show how well (or badly) a port is performing.
* ***Credible predictions*** — forecasts backed by “looking back, we were nn% on point.”
* ***Cargo flow analysis*** — bulk and container movements across the globe.
* ***AIS storytelling*** — auto-generate images or a short film of an incident for marketing.
* ***The agentic port*** — what does a port look like when agents run operations?
* ***Outward-facing data*** — something on our website that turns our data into a customer magnet.

## How we pick a winner

A small jury scores the demos against clear criteria so the result feels fair.

Suggested panel: one MT member, one maritime domain expert, and a “customer voice” to keep us honest about real-world value.

**Innovation** — how original and bold is the idea?  
**Smart use of data + AI** — does it make real use of Teqplay data and generative/agentic AI?  
**Impact** — would this actually help a customer or Teqplay?  
**Working demo** — does it run, even held together with tape?  
**Presentation** — can the team tell a clear, compelling story?

The prize: Medals for the winning team — plus year-long bragging rights and a trophy that travels to the next edition.
